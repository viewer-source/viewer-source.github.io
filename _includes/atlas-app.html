Edit the map shown.

To save moves to the site, paste a GitHub token that can write to this site's repository. It stays in this browser only.

This tool is designed for improving the layout of a map. Moves are saved by themselves; the site shows them about two minutes later. The map moves and zooms as outside edit mode: a drag on empty space moves it, two-finger scrolling or a pinch zooms, a right click opens the same menu. A click selects a node, an arc or a bend point, and a drag moves what is selected; a second click without a drag deselects it. Shift with a drag on empty space selects an area; Shift with a click adds to the selection. Arrow keys move by 1, with Shift by 10. The dotted square puts the whole map on the grid by the layout rules (complexes packed, rows and columns kept), one step to undo. The complex with two proteins packs the complex selected (or the one round a member selected), every complex when nothing is selected. With Snap to grid on (the magnet), the node dragged puts its centre on the 8 px grid and a bend point goes on 4 px (the layout rules' grid); Alt held, no snapping. Ctrl or Cmd with Z undoes, with S saves at once.
